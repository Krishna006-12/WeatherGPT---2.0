# WeatherGPT 2.0

**Weather intelligence that turns forecasts and live events into decisions.**

WeatherGPT 2.0 is a clean rebuild of WeatherGPT, designed around four principles:

1. Weather facts come from trusted weather/data providers — never from an LLM.
2. One normalized weather engine powers the entire product.
3. AI explains, compares, personalizes, and recommends; it does not fabricate live facts.
4. Live Weather Intelligence combines verified weather-related news, event context, forecast signals, and location-specific impact analysis.

## Core pillars

- Accurate current, hourly, and daily weather
- Grounded AI weather assistant
- Live Weather Intelligence / weather-related news events
- India and user-location impact analysis
- Severe-weather alerts and risk intelligence
- Multi-model forecast confidence (later phase)
- Agriculture, travel, school, voice, memory, and offline intelligence (later phases)

## Product idea

> WeatherGPT does not only tell you what the weather is. It explains what it means for you.

Example:

- Normal weather app: `Rain probability: 78%`
- WeatherGPT: `Rain risk rises after 4:30 PM. If travelling by bike, leave earlier. Avoid crop spraying after 2 PM.`
